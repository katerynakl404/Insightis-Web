Classes

ClassUse
.ins-bottom-ctaBase organism — glow card, top brand line, responsive flex layout. Default = form variant.
.ins-bottom-cta--buttonsCentered column: title + optional desc + primary/secondary button pair.
.ins-bottom-cta--textCentered column: title + a single CTA button (no form, no secondary).
.ins-bottom-cta--rowOne-line banner: lead (title + note) on the left, CTA on the right (desktop).
.ins-bottom-cta__titleHeadline. Scales up in the centered variants; shrinks to wrap in the row layout.
.ins-bottom-cta__highlightTeal-tinted inline span for the emphasized phrase in the title.
.ins-bottom-cta__descSupporting sentence between title and actions (buttons/text variants).
.ins-bottom-cta__formComposes .ins-input-group; holds the input + submit button rail.
.ins-bottom-cta__inputTransparent input inside the group (fixed ~280px on desktop).
.ins-bottom-cta__actionsFlex wrapper for one or two CTA buttons.
.ins-bottom-cta__leadRow variant only — stacks the title above its note.
.ins-bottom-cta__noteOne-line reassurance strip (plan, no-card, compliance).
