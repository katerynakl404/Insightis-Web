Scroll-spy TOC

Sticky "On this page" rail for long legal / docs pages. It mirrors the sidebar's title + rail styling; the link matching the section in view lights up teal (aria-current="location"). Live on Privacy, Terms and Cookie Settings (src/pages/privacy.jsx). Add data-sb-scrollspy to the container and storybook.js wires the IntersectionObserver that toggles the active link as you scroll.

On this page (live scroll-spy)

Data Collected

We collect the account details you provide and the operational metadata needed to run Insightis — never the contents of your connected warehouses beyond what a query returns.

Use of the Data

Data is used to operate, secure and improve the service, and to answer the questions you ask of your own data.

Who Has Access to the Data

Access is limited to the members of your workspace and a small set of Devart personnel bound by strict confidentiality controls.

Your Connected Data

Your warehouse stays in your warehouse. Insightis reads models on demand through the Semantic Layer and does not copy them wholesale.

AI Processing

Prompts and results are processed to generate answers and are not used to train third-party foundation models.

Where the Data is Stored

Operational data is stored on infrastructure in the region tied to your plan, encrypted at rest and in transit.

GDPR Compliance

We support the access, rectification and erasure rights afforded under the GDPR and equivalent regimes.

Sharing of Data

We do not sell your data. Limited processors are engaged only where necessary to deliver the service.
