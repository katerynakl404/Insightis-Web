Pain-Point Grid

Responsive grid of pain cards used in the "problem" section of Solutions pages. Each card pairs a red ✕ .ins-icon-badge--red with a title and body. The top edge carries a faint red-to-transparent accent line. Grid flows 3 → 2 → 1 columns.

Grid (3 cards)

Every metric has three definitions

Sales, finance and product each compute CAC their own way, so every review opens by arguing about whose number is right instead of what to do next.

Every answer is a SQL ticket

Business teams wait days for the data team to hand-write a query, and the analysts spend their week on the same long tail of one-off pulls.

Dashboards break silently

A renamed column upstream quietly poisons a report, and nobody notices until a decision has already been made on the wrong number.
