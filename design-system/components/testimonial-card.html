Testimonial card

A customer-quote card with a top brand-glow accent line and a hover-lit border. Two visual configurations (from src/components/TestimonialCard.jsx): the Solutions style — opening-quote SVG, quote, then a name/role footer with an optional mono company pill — used on the by-role solution pages; and the homepage style — a 5-star row, a decorative corner mark, and an avatar beside the name/role.

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-testimonialCard shell. Relative + overflow-hidden, dark translucent fill, hover-lit teal border, and an always-on top brand-glow line (::before).
.ins-testimonial--has-avatarModifier: turns the footer into a flex row (avatar beside name/role) and drops the footer's top divider.
.ins-testimonial__cornerDecorative &ldquo; mark pinned top-right (homepage idiom, defaults on when stars are shown).
.ins-testimonial__iconOpening-quote SVG (Solutions idiom). 22px, half-opacity, highlight colour.
.ins-testimonial__starsAmber 5-star row above the quote; each > svg is 13px.
.ins-testimonial__quoteQuote body. flex:1 so the footer sits at the card's foot regardless of length.
.ins-testimonial__footerFooter block with a top divider; the divider/padding are removed under --has-avatar.
.ins-testimonial__nameAttribution name (heading colour, medium weight).
.ins-testimonial__roleRole / title line under the name (inactive colour).
.ins-testimonial__companyOptional mono uppercase pill below the role (Solutions idiom).
.ins-testimonial__avatar<img> avatar circle, 36px, object-fit cover.
.ins-testimonial__avatar-fallbackInitials circle shown when no photo — teal gradient fill.
