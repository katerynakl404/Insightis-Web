ClassUse

.ins-progress--indeterminateMakes the bar sweep left-to-right on a loop.
.ins-typingInline row of three animated dots (chat "is typing").
.ins-typing__dotOne dot; children 2 and 3 are delayed for the wave.
.ins-typing--smCompact: no padding, 4.5px dots (inline label pairing).
