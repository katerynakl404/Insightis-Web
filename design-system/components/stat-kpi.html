Stat / KPI

Two related pieces. .ins-stat-kpi is a single figure — uppercase label, oversized tabular value, optional mono delta (positive/negative). .ins-stat-strip is the bordered "by the numbers" band that carries four of them across a row with vertical dividers. The strip is the live <StatStrip> on the homepage ("What you get with Insightis") and the About hero.

Single KPI

Label + value stack. The value drops to 36px on mobile, 48px from the md breakpoint, and uses tabular numerals so digits don't jitter.

Monthly churn 2.1%
MRR $284k 
<div class="ins-stat-kpi">
  <span class="ins-stat-kpi__label">Monthly churn</span>
  <span class="ins-stat-kpi__value">2.1%</span>
</div>

With delta

The mono __delta sits under the value. --positive uses the success color, --negative the error color. Pair with an inline arrow SVG.

Churn vs last month 2.1% 0.7pp lower
CAC vs last quarter $412 8% higher

  <span class="ins-stat-kpi__value">2.1%</span>

  </span>
</div>

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-stat-kpiOne figure: vertical label/value/delta stack.
.ins-stat-kpi__labelUppercase 11px inactive-color label.
.ins-stat-kpi__value36px (48px from md) 700-weight value, tight tracking, tabular numerals.
.ins-stat-kpi__deltaMono 12px inline delta with an arrow icon.
.ins-stat-kpi__delta--positiveSuccess color (improvement).
.ins-stat-kpi__delta--negativeError color (regression).
.ins-stat-stripThe "by the numbers" band (card.css): bordered surface, 2-col → 4-col grid with vertical dividers. Also a lighter KPI-flow layout in stat-kpi.css.
.ins-stat-strip__itemOne centered column inside the band.
.ins-stat-strip__valueTeal 36px/48px count-up figure.
.ins-stat-strip__labelHeading-color label under the value.
.ins-stat-strip__subOptional 12px body sub-label.
