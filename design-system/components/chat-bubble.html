Chat Bubble

Conversation bubbles for the AI Chat surface. User bubbles are teal-tinted and align right; AI bubbles use the card surface and align left; a compact typing bubble holds the loading dots. Live in the AI Chat page (src/pages/ai-chat.jsx) — both the questions gallery and the animated hero demo. The dots come from the .ins-typing loader (loaders.css).

Conversation

A real exchange from the questions gallery — the user asks in plain English, Insightis answers grounded in live data.

Why did MRR drop last week?
Trial expiry without activation drove 60% of last week’s $1,240 MRR drop. Six distinct causes identified — the largest being trial expiry ($740) and Pro → Starter downgrades ($260).
Which deals risk slipping?
7 deals totalling $184K are at risk of slipping past Q2. NovaCorp ($52K) has been untouched for 21 days — I’d prioritise an executive business review there first.

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-chatVertical stack of bubbles with a consistent gap.
.ins-chat__bubbleBase bubble — max 80% width, rounded, bordered, preserves line breaks.
.ins-chat__bubble--userUser message — teal tint, right-aligned, notched bottom-right corner.
.ins-chat__bubble--aiAI message — card surface, left-aligned, notched bottom-left corner.
.ins-chat__bubble--typingCompact loading bubble; tightens padding and the enclosed .ins-typing dots.
.ins-typing / .ins-typing__dotThree animated dots (from loaders.css) placed inside a typing bubble or next to a label.
.ins-typing--smSmaller dots with no padding — inline "Thinking…" use.
.ins-typing--brandBrand-tinted (teal) dots.
