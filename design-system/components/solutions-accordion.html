Solutions accordion

The home page "Made for every team" section (src/components/SolutionsAccordion.jsx): a row of photo panels, one expanded at a time. It is a single-select disclosure set — a collapsed panel shows just its one-word label at the foot of the strip, and the open panel reveals a description and a "Solutions for …" link once its width has settled. Below lg it is replaced by a stack of full-bleed .ins-solacc-card links.

Accordion — one panel open

Interactive open/close is driven by React in production. This static specimen fixes the first panel open via the data-open attribute; the collapsed panels keep their region hidden. Panels use the --placeholder gradient in place of the shipped WebP photos.

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-solaccDesktop accordion row (460px tall). Flex is applied by the JSX (lg:flex) — supply display:flex yourself outside that context.
.ins-solacc__panelOne photo panel. Same flex-basis on every panel; only flex-grow animates. [data-open] grows it and lights the teal border.
.ins-solacc__img / --placeholderAbsolutely-positioned cover photo; collapsed panels are desaturated and dimmed. The --placeholder variant is a brand-tinted gradient for panels with no photo.
.ins-solacc__scrimContrast veil + bottom fade so text clears AA over any photo.
.ins-solacc__bodyContent layer pinned to the foot of the panel; fills the panel when collapsed so the label sits at the bottom.
.ins-solacc__hd / __triggerHeading wrapper and the full-panel <button> trigger (its ::after stretches the hit area over the whole tile). Larger type when open.
.ins-solacc__labelOne-word collapsed label (block, text-wrap:balance).
.ins-solacc__regionDisclosure region revealed on open; kept in the DOM with hidden when collapsed.
.ins-solacc__descDescription paragraph (white at 82% for legibility over photos).
.ins-solacc__link / __arrow"Solutions for …" link and its arrow icon (nudges on hover/focus).
.ins-solacc__ctaOptional bottom-right container for a secondary CTA button.
.ins-solacc-card / __img / __body / __titleThe below-lg stacked card: whole card is a link, photo behind a gradient floor, title + reused .ins-solacc__desc/__link.
