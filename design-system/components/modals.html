Component

Modal

Centered overlay dialog with a blurred scrim. Three widths (sm 400px, default 560px, lg 800px) plus a right-edge drawer. The live "Request a connector" flow (src/components/RequestConnectorModal.jsx) is an --sm dialog. Open/close, ESC and outside-click are wired by storybook.js via data-sb-open / data-sb-close.

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-modalFixed full-screen scrim; centers the dialog and blurs the page behind it.
.ins-modal[aria-hidden="true"]Hidden state — the storybook toggles this attribute to show/hide.
.ins-modal__dialogThe card itself (560px default): surface, border, 2xl radius, large shadow.
.ins-modal--smNarrows the dialog to 400px — confirmations and the live connector-request form.
.ins-modal--lgWidens the dialog to 800px — complex forms and full workflows.
.ins-modal--drawerSlides a 360px full-height panel in from the right (mobile menu); rounds only the left corners.
.ins-modal__headerTitle row with bottom border; holds the close button.
.ins-modal__title18px semibold heading.
.ins-modal__bodyScrollable content area (flexes to fill).
.ins-modal__footerAction row; column-reverse on mobile, right-aligned row ≥768px.
