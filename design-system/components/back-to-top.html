Component

Back to Top

Floating circular button pinned to the bottom-right of every page. It is position:fixed and hidden (opacity 0, inert) until public/back-to-top.js adds .is-visible past 400px of scroll; it lifts clear of the cookie notice while that bar is up.

Visible state

<!-- Built by public/back-to-top.js and appended to <body>.
     .is-visible is toggled on once scrollY > 400px. -->
<button type="button" class="ins-to-top is-visible" aria-label="Back to top">
  <svg width="18" height="18" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true">
    <line x1="12" y1="19" x2="12" y2="5"/><polyline points="5 12 12 5 19 12"/>
  </svg>
</button>

Hidden state

Without .is-visible the button is invisible and inert (nothing paints in this stage).

<!-- Default: hidden until the scroll script promotes it. -->
<button type="button" class="ins-to-top" aria-label="Back to top">…</button>

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-to-topFixed 44px circular button, bottom-right. Hidden and inert by default (opacity 0, pointer-events none).
.ins-to-top.is-visibleRevealed state toggled by the scroll script past 400px (opacity 1, interactive).
.has-ins-cookie-notice .ins-to-topGlobal modifier (on <html>) that lifts the button clear of the consent bar while it is up.
