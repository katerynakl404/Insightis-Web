Tooltip

CSS-only hover/focus label. Wrap a trigger in .ins-tooltip-wrap and add a positioned .ins-tooltip sibling — it fades in on :hover or :focus-within. Four placements plus a --rich title+description variant. Hover a trigger below to reveal it.

Placements

top · right · bottom · left. Hover or tab to each button.

Rich (title + description)

--rich allows wrapping text up to 240px; pair with __title and __description. Ideal for explaining a metric definition on hover.

Monthly Recurring Revenue
Normalized to the calendar month, excluding one-time charges and taxes.
<div class="ins-tooltip-wrap">
  <button class="ins-btn ins-btn--icon" aria-label="What is MRR?"><svg>…</svg></button>
  <div class="ins-tooltip ins-tooltip--top ins-tooltip--rich" role="tooltip">
    <div class="ins-tooltip__title">Monthly Recurring Revenue</div>
    <div class="ins-tooltip__description">Normalized to the calendar month, excluding one-time charges and taxes.</div>
  </div>
</div>

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-tooltip-wrapRelative wrapper around the trigger; provides the hover/focus anchor.
.ins-tooltipThe bubble: elevated surface, 12px text, hidden until hover/focus (fades opacity).
.ins-tooltip--topPositions above the trigger, centered.
.ins-tooltip--bottomPositions below the trigger, centered.
.ins-tooltip--leftPositions to the left, vertically centered.
.ins-tooltip--rightPositions to the right, vertically centered.
.ins-tooltip--richAllows wrapping (max 240px) for a title + description.
.ins-tooltip__titleBold heading inside a rich tooltip.
.ins-tooltip__descriptionMuted body line inside a rich tooltip.
