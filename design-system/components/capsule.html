Capsule

The floating pill shell shared by the site header and the cookie consent notice: a bordered, rounded, shadowed bar inset from the viewport edges. It is a composed primitive — it deliberately carries no fill, blur, or position of its own, so each consumer (.ins-cookie-notice adds a solid grey fill; the header adds translucent glass) supplies those itself.

Real consumers

In production the capsule is never used alone. Two blocks compose it:

  • .ins-capsule.ins-cookie-notice — the fixed-bottom consent bar (solid grey fill).
  • the site header — sticky, translucent glass fill with a prefixed-first backdrop blur.
