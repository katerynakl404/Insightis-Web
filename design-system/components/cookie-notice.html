Cookie notice

The implied-consent bar built by public/cookie-notice.js and shown at the foot of every page until dismissed. It composes .ins-capsule — the floating pill shell it shares with the site header — and adds only a solid grey fill, a text/link/button row, and its fixed-bottom positioning. The script sets both classes on one node; dismissal writes a localStorage flag so a returning visitor never sees a flash.

Consent bar

In production the bar is position:fixed to the bottom of the viewport. For this specimen only, it is overridden to position:relative so it renders inside the stage. Everything else — the capsule shell, grey fill, and flex row — is the real component.

We use cookies on this website. By using this site you agree that we may store and access cookies on your device as explained in our Cookies Policy.

<!-- Built by public/cookie-notice.js. Both classes on one node:
     .ins-capsule supplies the shell, .ins-cookie-notice the grey fill + fixed-bottom position. -->
<div class="ins-capsule ins-cookie-notice" role="region" aria-label="Cookie notice">
  <p class="ins-cookie-notice__text">
    We use cookies on this website. By using this site you agree that we may store and
    access cookies on your device as explained in our
    <a class="ins-cookie-notice__link" href="/security/cookie-settings">Cookies Policy</a>.
  </p>
  <button type="button" class="ins-cookie-notice__btn" aria-label="Dismiss cookie notice">Got It</button>
</div>

Behaviour & layout notes

  • Fixed at bottom: var(--ins-space-md) with z-index: var(--ins-z-sticky); grey --ins-surface-elevated fill (solid, not glass, so it reads as a distinct panel over the near-black page).
  • While the bar is up, the script adds .has-ins-cookie-notice to <html>, which pads the body bottom so the bar can't cover the footer's links.
  • Below 768px the capsule wraps, drops to a rounded-card radius, and the button goes full-width.
  • role="region" (not alertdialog): it is non-modal and must not steal focus. Esc dismisses only while focus is inside the bar.
<!-- html gets this class while the bar is present (reserves footer space) -->
