Tabs

Keyboard-navigable tab set. The underline default marks the active tab with a teal border-bottom; --pill swaps to a rounded pill group; --vertical stacks the list on the left with a teal left border. storybook.js wires any .ins-tabs — click or arrow-key a .ins-tabs__tab and it selects the matching .ins-tabs__panel by index.

Underline (default)

The active tab carries aria-selected="true"; inactive panels are hidden. Arrow keys, Home and End move focus.

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-tabsRoot. storybook.js binds click + arrow/Home/End keys here.
.ins-tabs__listFlex row of tabs with a bottom border; horizontally scrollable, scrollbar hidden.
.ins-tabs__tabOne tab button. Active via [aria-selected="true"] or .is-active — teal text + underline.
.ins-tabs__panelPanel body; hide inactive panels with the hidden attribute.
.ins-tabs--pillRounded bordered pill group; active tab gets an elevated surface, no underline.
.ins-tabs--verticalLeft-stacked list with a left border; active tab gets a teal left border.
