Comparison Cards

Side-by-side before/after panels used in the "Why Insightis" section of the homepage and Solutions pages. The --before panel is a red error-glow card with ✕ marks; the --after panel is a teal brand-glow card with ✓ marks and a top brand line. Each panel has a dot-label header over a checklist.

Before / After

Traditional Approach

  • ✕Siloed tools & fragmented views
  • ✕High ongoing engineering overhead
With Insightis
  • ✓Automated data unification
  • ✓Natural language — no SQL needed
  • ✓Instant reports, zero code
  • ✓Unified intelligence layer
  • ✓10× more cost-effective

Classes

ClassUse
.ins-compareResponsive grid — single column on mobile, two equal columns at ≥768px.
.ins-compare__panelBase panel — padding + large radius. Always combined with a tone modifier.
.ins-compare__panel--beforeRed "before" tone — error background, red border, error glow.
.ins-compare__panel--afterTeal "after" tone — brand tint, brand border, brand glow + top brand line.
.ins-compare__headerDot-label header; colored red (before) or teal (after).
.ins-compare__dot8px status dot in the header (glows on the after panel).
.ins-compare__listReset <ul> — vertical checklist stack.
.ins-compare__itemOne checklist row: mark + text.
.ins-compare__markLeading ✕ (before) / ✓ (after) mark, tinted to the panel tone.
