The grid and the billing toggle are built with inline styles in the real page, not DS layout classes — reproduced verbatim here. The toggle leads with Yearly (the default cycle and quoted price); switching to Monthly swaps every card's headline/struck figures and hides the "billed annually" line. Only the tier surface (.ins-card--pricing / .ins-card--selected) and the CTA (.ins-btn) are DS classes.

Classes

ClassRole
.ins-cardBase card surface.
.ins-card--pricingPricing variant — flex column, body flexes, CTA aligns to baseline.
.ins-card--selectedFeatured tier — brand border + brand-tint surface (Pro).
.ins-btn / --primary / --secondary / --mdPer-tier CTA button (primary on the featured tier).
[data-pricing-grid]Inline 3-col grid hook (not a DS class); collapses to 1 col ≤900px.
role="tablist" / role="tab"Inline billing toggle (BillingToggle.jsx) — no DS segmented class.

Usage. Source src/pages/pricing.jsx with plan data from src/data/pricing.js, the toggle from src/components/BillingToggle.jsx and the card shell from src/components/Card.jsx (CSS ds/components/card.css). Prices, features and the 50% launch badge are the real yearly-cycle figures.
