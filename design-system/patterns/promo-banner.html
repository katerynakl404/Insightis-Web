Classes

ClassUse
.ins-bottom-ctaBase organism — glow card, top brand line, responsive flex layout.
.ins-bottom-cta--rowOne-line banner variant: lead on the left, CTA on the right (desktop); stacks on mobile.
.ins-bottom-cta__leadLeft column — stacks the title above its note.
.ins-bottom-cta__titleBanner headline. Shrinks to wrap so it never clips the card at ≥768px.
.ins-bottom-cta__highlightTeal-tinted inline span for the emphasized phrase in the title.
.ins-bottom-cta__noteOne-line reassurance strip under the title (plan, no-card, compliance).
.ins-bottom-cta__actionsRight-side flex wrapper holding the CTA button.
.ins-btn .ins-btn--primary .ins-btn--lgThe CTA button (anchor).

Usage. Rendered by src/components/BottomCTA.jsx with variant="row"; the trustNote prop becomes .ins-bottom-cta__note and <BottomCTA.Highlight> becomes the highlight span. The row copy shown here is the compact alternative documented in src/main.jsx (BottomCTASection).
