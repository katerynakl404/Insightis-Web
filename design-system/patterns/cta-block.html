Classes

ClassUse
.ins-bottom-ctaBase organism — glow card, top brand line, responsive flex layout.
.ins-bottom-cta--textCentered column variant: title + optional desc + CTA(s), stacked and centered.
.ins-bottom-cta__titleHeadline. Scales up (28→36px) in the centered variant.
.ins-bottom-cta__highlightTeal-tinted inline span for the emphasized phrase in the title.
.ins-bottom-cta__descSupporting sentence between the title and the actions.
.ins-bottom-cta__actionsFlex wrapper that centers one or two CTA buttons.
.ins-bottom-cta__noteOptional one-line reassurance strip under the actions.
.ins-btn .ins-btn--primary .ins-btn--lgPrimary CTA button (anchor).
.ins-btn .ins-btn--secondary .ins-btn--lgSecondary CTA button (anchor).

Usage. Rendered by src/components/BottomCTA.jsx with variant="text" (single CTA) or variant="buttons" for the primary/secondary pair; the highlight is <BottomCTA.Highlight>. The mid-page reuse lives in src/components/MidCTA.jsx.
